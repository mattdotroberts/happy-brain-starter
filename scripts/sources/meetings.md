# Meeting notes and transcripts

The only record of what was decided out loud. Most of what is agreed in a
meeting is written down nowhere else.

## Access

A notetaker that records and transcribes, with an API or a local export.

## Fetch

New meetings since the watermark, with the transcript, not just the summary. The
summary is someone else's judgement about what mattered.

## Write

`sources/meetings/YYYY-MM-DD/<meeting-slug>.md` with attendees in the frontmatter.

## Noise

Very little. A meeting that happened is worth recording even if nothing came of
it, because "we discussed this in March" is a question people ask.

## Gotchas

- Transcripts misspell names constantly. Resolve to the person page and note the
  misspelling once rather than creating a second page.
- Decisions hide in asides. Read the whole thing rather than the headline.
