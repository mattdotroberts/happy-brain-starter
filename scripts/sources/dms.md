# Professional DMs (LinkedIn, X, Slack)

> **A known-good setup** is listed in [`docs/what-i-use.md`](../../docs/what-i-use.md), with what breaks.

Low volume, high value. Introductions and opportunities arrive here and then get
forgotten, because these inboxes are not checked like email.

## Access

A bridge or connector that exposes the conversations locally. Native APIs for
these platforms are either closed or heavily rate limited, so a desktop bridge
is usually the practical route.

## Fetch

Since the watermark, per conversation. Volume is small enough to take everything
and judge afterwards.

## Write

`sources/dms/YYYY-MM-DD/<who-and-what>.md`, one file per conversation per day.

## Noise

Automated outreach, recruiter spam, "congratulations on your work anniversary".

## Gotchas

- These platforms block scraping aggressively. If you find yourself fetching
  twenty profiles in a loop, stop: you will trip a bot check and risk the account.
- An unanswered DM is itself a fact. The ledger should notice silence.
