# Saved reading (bookmarks, watched videos, articles)

> **A known-good setup** is listed in [`docs/what-i-use.md`](../../docs/what-i-use.md), with what breaks.

Not operational. This builds a reference library: why you believe what you
believe, traceable months later.

## Access

Bookmark exports, a watch history, or a read-later service.

## Fetch

New items since the watermark, deduplicated by id. A second watch is an event,
not a second item.

For video, a transcript is required for a complete ingest. Prefer creator
captions, then automatic captions. **Never write up a video from its title.**

## Write

`sources/reading/YYYY-MM-DD/<id>.md`, with both links preserved: the archived
copy and the original. A citation that cannot take you back to both is incomplete.

## Noise

Sport, music, entertainment, anything watched for fun. Keep professional
learning broadly: technology, business, product, psychology, economics.

## Gotchas

- **Do not create a page per item.** Two independent useful sources, or one
  unusually important one, earns a topic page. Update a synthesis rather than
  piling up summaries.
- Reference material never creates commitments.
